Your lantern flickers inside the dark cave. Gold coins cover the floor, jeweled goblets lie between the rocks, and open chests overflow with treasure. Somewhere beyond the darkness, waves break against the island.

Your ship waits offshore. The skiff you left on the beach is your way back, but first you must get out of the cave.

The treasure is within reach. So is the passage toward the exit.
