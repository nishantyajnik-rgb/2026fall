Back Into Darkness

You clutch the treasure against your chest and try to force your way past the skeletons. They surround you before you reach the opening.

A rusted blade strikes your lantern. The flame goes out. You hear coins falling, then nothing.

When the light returns, you are standing deep inside the cave again. The chests are open. Your pockets are empty. The treasure lies all around you, exactly where it was before. The curse has sent you back to the start.
