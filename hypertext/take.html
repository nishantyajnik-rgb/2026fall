A Handful of Gold

You fill your pockets with coins and lift a jeweled goblet from an open chest. The weight feels reassuring as you move toward the exit.

Then your lantern catches a bone-white hand. Skeletons pull themselves from the shadows, their empty eye sockets fixed on the treasure you carry. Rusted blades scrape against the cave walls.

One lunges at you. Another blocks your path. You can drop the treasure and keep running, or hold onto it and try to push through.
